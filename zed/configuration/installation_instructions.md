TestingBot runs your tests on real browsers and real iOS/Android devices. You need a TestingBot account (free trial at [testingbot.com](https://testingbot.com)).

You don't have to enter a key: leave both fields empty and ask the agent to sign in to TestingBot. It runs the `tb_login` tool, which opens a sign-in page in your browser and saves the credentials in `~/.testingbot/credentials`.

To use a key instead, copy the key and secret from [your account page](https://testingbot.com/members/user/security).
